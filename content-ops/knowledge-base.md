# Calibre Squad Content Knowledge Base

**Last reviewed:** 17 September 2026

## Calibre Squad

Calibre Squad is a Western Australian consultancy and product studio based in Perth. It provides consultancy, operational resources and documentation, and purpose-built apps. The public positioning is practical, precise work designed for real operational use.

Primary website: https://calibresquad.com.au/

## AuraMix

AuraMix is a Calibre Squad product in TestFlight beta. It is a precision equaliser and audio toolkit for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Publicly stated features include signature presets, a live 54-band spectrum, and a player built around the user's own music library.

Product page: https://calibresquad.com.au/auramix/

### Approved factual claims

- Built by Calibre Squad in Perth, Western Australia.
- Designed for iPhone, iPad and Mac.
- Includes equalisation tools, signature presets, a live 54-band spectrum, and a music player for the user's own library.
- Currently described publicly as a TestFlight beta.

### Claims requiring owner evidence before use

- Audio-quality improvements or superiority over named alternatives.
- Compatibility with specific streaming services, formats, devices or headphones.
- Pricing, App Store release date, beta capacity, and geographic availability.
- Measured performance, battery impact, latency, privacy, and accessibility claims.
- Testimonials, usage numbers, conversion rates, rankings, or awards.

### Standard disclosure

> I am building AuraMix through Calibre Squad, so I am connected to the product.

Short form when the relationship is already clear:

> Disclosure: I am part of the team building AuraMix.

## HVA Comply

HVA Comply is a Calibre Squad web compliance product for Western Australian heavy-vehicle operators. It is outside the first Reddit pilot unless the owner explicitly expands the watchlist.

## Voice

- Human, practical and specific.
- Helpful before promotional.
- Comfortable acknowledging limitations and trade-offs.
- Avoid corporate filler, exaggerated claims, engagement bait and artificial enthusiasm.
- Use Australian English.

## Unknowns to confirm before product promotion

- AuraMix pricing model.
- Target public-release date.
- TestFlight invitation process and capacity.
- Supported music sources, audio formats and platform versions.
- Whether the app was substantially built with AI-generated code for communities that require such disclosure.
