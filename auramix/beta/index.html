How it works

  1. 01

    Send us your details

    Name, email and phone, plus what you'd like to test on. That's all we need.

  2. 02

    We send the invite

    Usually within a day. It arrives from Apple at the address you gave us.

  3. 03

    Install TestFlight

    A free Apple app. Open the invite on your device, tap Accept, and AuraMix installs alongside your other apps.

Beta builds are pre-release and can misbehave. Nothing you send us is used for anything but getting you an invite and replying to you — see the privacy policy. Questions before you commit? The support page has them.
